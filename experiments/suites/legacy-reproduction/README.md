the original experiment fixtures and submission provenance were not available
for independent verification. They preserve the earlier simplifying
assumptions by presenting a single author-derived influence context and
co-locating read authority with authorship. They must not be labelled as
canonical Conflux semantics or used to validate archived numerical results.
