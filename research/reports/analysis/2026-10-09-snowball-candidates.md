      + 2 * (both directions seen)                # reachable backwards and forwards (max 2)

## Method and limitations

- Backward = the seed's reference list; forward = works citing the seed. Provider order is OpenAlex, then Semantic Scholar, then (for DOI seeds, backward only) the Crossref `reference` array.
- A provider that is rate-limited, blocked or empty hands over to the next provider in the plan, so one exhausted quota does not drop a seed.
- Identity resolution order: corpus DOI or arXiv id, then a DOI/arXiv extracted from `source_url`, then a title search (OpenAlex, then the Semantic Scholar match endpoint, then a Crossref bibliographic search). A title hit is accepted only when the title is near-exact and the record is confirmed by an agreeing year or an overlapping author surname; a corpus title that differs from the published title stays unresolved on purpose.
- Dedupe checks DOI, arXiv id and normalised title against the 176 corpus keys; `possible_duplicate` means a title-similarity score of at least 0.93 with no identifier match.
- The resolved work is reduced to a summary (title, year, DOI, OpenAlex type); the raw provider responses stay in the response cache keyed by URL and are not copied into the artefact.
- Citation counts and venue strings come from OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar and are metadata, not verified bibliography.
- Semantic Scholar is key-free and unauthenticated, so it is rate-limited hard: page fetches back off, and an empty reference list means a coverage gap, not an error.
- Crossref reference entries often carry no DOI and never carry a citation count, so those candidates rank lower and are excluded from the proposed entries.
- Forward traversal is capped per seed. `capped` in the machine-readable record means the cap stopped the traversal; `notes` records provider coverage gaps (for example a Crossref reference entry without a title, or an empty Semantic Scholar reference list).
- Raw graph hits that cannot be cited are dropped from the ranked list: no year and no DOI/arXiv/OpenAlex identity 243, reference entry names a venue or container, not a paper 44.
- Proposed entries are unverified proposals: admission to the corpus, the fetch manifest and `fetch_status.json` is a separate reviewed step.
